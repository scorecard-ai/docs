Scorecard supports SAML 2.0 single sign-on with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD), Google Workspace, and any other SAML 2.0 identity provider. SSO is included in the Enterprise plan. Sign-in is handled by [Clerk](https://clerk.com).

## Set up SSO

1. Email [support@scorecard.io](mailto:support@scorecard.io) with your organization name, your email domain, and your identity provider.
2. Scorecard sends the service provider details for your identity provider, and your IT team sends back the SAML metadata.
3. Scorecard configures the connection, tests it with you, and turns it on.

## Signing in

People with an email on your domain enter it on the Scorecard sign-in page and are sent to your identity provider. The first time someone signs in through SSO, their Scorecard account is created for them.

Admins still manage roles inside Scorecard. See [Organizations and projects](/features/projects#members-and-roles).
