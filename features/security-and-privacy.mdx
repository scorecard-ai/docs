## Data Encryption and Data Security

Scorecard AI takes extensive measures to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and overall security of your data.

* **Encryption:** Data is stored encrypted (using the AES-256 standard) and transmitted encrypted (using HTTPS/TLS protocols). This protects sensitive details such as access tokens and confidential information.
* **Data Erasure:** We erase data upon request or when it reaches its designated retention period, ensuring that outdated information isn’t stored unnecessarily.
* **Physical Security:** Our cloud service providers ensure the physical security of the infrastructure where your data is stored using industry-standard encryption.

## Truffle (AI Assistant)

Truffle, Scorecard's in-app assistant, answers your questions with a large language model. To do that, it sends your message and the parts of your project it needs, like the record you're looking at and its trace, to a model provider through [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai).

* **No Retention by Model Providers:** We only allow providers with a zero data retention policy. They don't store your data after the request, and they don't train on it.
* **Provider and Region Can Vary:** OpenRouter picks among the allowed providers for each request, so we don't guarantee a specific provider or region.
* **Your Conversations Stay in Scorecard:** We store your Truffle chats so you can pick them back up later. Deleting your organization deletes them.
